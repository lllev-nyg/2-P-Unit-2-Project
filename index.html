Arch Manning was born on April 27, 2004. he is 22 and he is the Starting Quarterback for the University of Texas. He plays with Cam Coleman a wide reciever. Who is a projected 1st round pick in 2027, and one of the best Wide Recievers in College Football.

Manning is the son of Cooper Manning and he is the nephew of Super Bowl winning quarterbacks Peyton and Eli Manning.

Arch Manning is a projected 1st round pick in the upcoming 2027 NFL Draft on 4-27-27.

Arch Manning played high school football at Newman High School in Louisiana. And was one of their best quarterbacks ever in the schools history.
