Arch Manning is the son of Cooper Manning and he is the nephew of Super Bowl winning quarterbacks Peyton and Eli Manning.

Arch Manning is a projected 1st round pick like Cam Coleman in the upcoming 2027 NFL Draft on 4-27-27.

Arch Manning played high school football at Newman High School in Louisiana. And was one of their best quarterbacks ever in the schools history. Arch Manning has totalled 4,793 passing yards and 42 passing touchdowns and 14 rushing touchdowns so far in his three years of college.
